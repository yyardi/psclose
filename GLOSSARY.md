# Glossary

A professional-services (PS) business sells and delivers people's time and
expertise — implementation, configuration, advisory, and similar work —
rather than, or alongside, a product. This file defines general PS and
accounting vocabulary only; it says nothing about this client's contracts,
data, or which mechanics are actually present here.

## Commercial documents

- **Sales order (SO).** The customer-approved order capturing what was
  sold, to whom, and for how much — the commercial record that authorizes
  delivery and billing.
- **Contract item.** A single priced line within a sales order representing
  one unit of what's being sold; typically the level at which revenue is
  tracked and reported.
- **Change request (CR).** A documented change to a contract's scope,
  hours, or price after the original agreement was signed. CRs vary in
  commercial substance — some carry a price and change contract value,
  others just reallocate hours or scope without a price attached.

## Delivery & operations

- **Timesheet entry.** A record of hours a team member logged against a
  project on a given day, tagged with a service, a task type, and an
  approval status.
- **Billable vs. non-billable.** Whether hours logged are chargeable to
  the customer under the contract. Non-billable hours still represent
  real delivery effort.
- **Percent complete / POC (percentage-of-completion).** An estimate of
  how much of an engagement's total scope has been delivered, expressed
  as a percentage.

## Finance & accounting

- **Revenue recognition.** The accounting process of recording revenue in
  the period it's earned, which isn't necessarily the period it's
  invoiced or collected.
- **Recognized vs. planned amount.** The planned amount is what a
  contract item is expected to earn in a period; the recognized amount is
  what was actually recorded as earned revenue for that period.
- **Cumulative vs. periodic amounts.** A periodic amount covers one
  reporting period; a cumulative amount is the running total from
  contract inception through that period. A period's own activity is the
  difference between two consecutive cumulative figures.
- **Revenue schedule.** A record, typically one row per contract item per
  period, showing the planned and recognized revenue for that item and
  period, plus a status such as open or finalized/posted.
- **Posting / journal entry (JE).** An accounting-system record that
  books an amount to the general ledger. Posting kinds:
  - *Opening* — a posted balance representing everything recognized for a
    contract item before a reporting window begins.
  - *Actual* — a normal period posting recording revenue recognized in
    that period through ordinary processing.
  - *Manual JE* — a posting entered directly by an accountant outside the
    normal automated flow, typically to correct or true up a balance.
  - *Credit* — a posting that reduces previously recognized revenue,
    usually from a credit memo, return, or reversal.
- **Credit memo.** A document that reduces amounts owed or previously
  recognized, issued when a customer is owed money back.
- **RMA / return.** A formal record of goods, services, or contract value
  being returned or reversed after being sold or delivered.
- **T&M vs. fixed fee vs. subscription billing.** T&M bills, and
  typically recognizes, based on hours or effort delivered. Fixed fee
  bills a set price for a defined scope, typically recognized against
  percent-complete or milestones. Subscription bills and recognizes
  ratably over a service term.
- **Month-end close.** The process of finalizing a period's transactions
  and postings so reported figures for that period become fixed, absent
  a formal correction.
